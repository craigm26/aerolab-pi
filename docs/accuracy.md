# Virtual flight accuracy report

Generated by `python tools/virtual_flight.py --markdown docs/accuracy.md`.
The real daemon ran, as a separate process, against simulated serial sensors speaking each device's protocol, with known truth.
The simulated installation had deliberate errors: IMU bias (+0.8° roll, -0.5° pitch), laser offset 0.62 m against 0.40 m entered by hand, laser boresight 1.0° in roll, 2% corrupted frames, 1% laser spikes, 3 cm laser noise, 0.4 m GNSS noise.
Profiles with an attitude source fly ±20° banks every 16 s at 55 m/s; profiles without one (attitude in post-processing) fly straight legs (±3°). Height is the profile's survey height, capped at 80% of the laser's rated range.

Every self-test says **warn** on purpose: the aircraft had not been calibrated at boot, and for checksummed protocols it caught the injected corrupted frames. "AGL p95" for attitude profiles is after ground calibration and the boresight fit. The GRF-500 reports in 0.1 m steps, hence its 0.6 m offset. Laser readings logged counts every reading saved against the clean frames sent while the daemon was reading; lasers without a checksum (ILM-150-R, Riegl, generic ASCII) also log the lines corrupted on the wire (kept out of AGL by the spike gate), so their count can exceed the clean frames.

# Virtual flight accuracy report

Generated by `python tools/virtual_flight.py --markdown docs/accuracy.md`.
The real daemon ran against simulated serial sensors speaking each device's protocol, with known truth.
The simulated installation had deliberate errors: IMU bias (+0.8° roll, -0.5° pitch), laser offset 0.62 m against 0.40 m entered by hand, laser boresight 1.0° in roll, 2% corrupted frames, 1% laser spikes, 3 cm laser noise. Flight: survey height, ±20° banks every 16 s, 55 m/s.

Every self-test says **warn** on purpose: it caught the injected corrupted frames (bad-frame rate above 1%) and that the aircraft had not been calibrated at boot. "AGL p95 before boresight" is after ground calibration but before the boresight fit; the remaining error there is the 1° laser tilt, which only banks reveal. The GRF-500 reports in 0.1 m steps, hence its 0.6 m offset.
